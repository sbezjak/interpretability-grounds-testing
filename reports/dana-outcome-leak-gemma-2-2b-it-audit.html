4. Method

  • Measure: expected blame - the average of 0-9, weighted by the model's probability for each digit. No judge model.
  • Locate: activation patching - swap the model's notes on the last word between the two stories, one layer at a time, both ways.
  • Mitigate: directional ablation - subtract one direction from the notes after layer 17, on every word. The direction = the average harm minus no-harm difference over two unrelated stories (nurse, engineer).
  • Validate: a held-out story, three unseen stories, a control where only the choice differs, 10 random directions.

5. Limitations

  • One 2B model; findings do not transfer to other models, including Claude or GPT.
  • Two build stories for the direction; about 30% of the bias survives removal.
  • Two qualifying test stories, 10 samples each: examples, not a rate; their confidence intervals are rough.
  • One unseen story's pass rule was clarified after a smoke run, before the main run.
  • Direction built on 0-9 prompts, applied unchanged to the 0-10 standard test.
